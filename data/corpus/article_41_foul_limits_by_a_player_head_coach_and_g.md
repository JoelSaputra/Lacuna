# Article 41 — Foul limits by a player/head coach and game disqualifications

41.1 A player who has committed 5 fouls shall be informed of it by a referee and must leave the game immediately. 

41.2 A player head coach who has committed 5 fouls as a player is an excluded player but may continue as a head coach. 

41.3 A foul by a player who has previously committed 5 fouls is an excluded player’s foul and it is charged and entered on the scoresheet against the head coach ('B'). 

41.4 A player who has committed two category 1 technical fouls or two flagrant fouls or one flagrant foul and one category 1 technical foul shall be informed of the game disqualification by the timer who shall raise the game disqualification (GD) marker. 

41.5 A head coach who has committed 2 technical fouls ('C') because of the personal unsportsmanlike behaviour or 3 technical fouls, either all of them ('B') or ('BD') or one of them ('C') shall be informed of the game disqualification by the timer who shall raise the game disqualification (GD) marker. 

41.6 Any disqualified player, substitute, head coach, assistant coach, excluded player or accompanying delegation member must leave the game immediately (taking no more than 30 seconds) and shall go to and remain in the team's dressing room for the duration of the game or shall leave the building.
