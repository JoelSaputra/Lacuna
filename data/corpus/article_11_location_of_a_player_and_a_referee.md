# Article 11 — Location of a player and a referee

11.1 The location of a player is determined by where that player is touching the floor. While airborne, the player shall retain the same status as where that player last touched the floor. This includes the boundary line, the centre line, the 3-point line, the free-throw line, the lines delimiting the restricted area and the lines delimiting the no-charge semi-circle area. 

11.2 The location of a referee is determined in the same manner as that of a player. When the ball touches a referee, it is the same as touching the floor at the referee's location.
