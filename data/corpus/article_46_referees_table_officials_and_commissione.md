# Article 46 — Referees, table officials and commissioner

46.1 The referees shall be a crew chief and 1 or 2 umpire(s). They shall be assisted by the table officials and by a commissioner, if present. 

46.2 The table officials shall be a scorer, an assistant scorer, a timer and a shot clock operator. 

46.3 The commissioner, if present, shall sit between the scorer and the timer. The commissioner’s primary duty during the game is to supervise the work of the table officials and to assist the crew chief and umpire(s) in the smooth functioning of the game. 

46.4 The referees of a given game should not be connected in any way with either team on the court. 

46.5 The referees, the table officials and the commissioner, if present, shall conduct the game in accordance with these rules and have no authority to change them. 

46.6 The referees' uniform shall consist of an referees’ shirt, long black trousers, black socks and black basketball shoes. 

46.7 The referees and table officials shall be uniformly dressed.
