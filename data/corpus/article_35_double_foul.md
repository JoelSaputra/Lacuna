# Article 35 — Double foul

35.1 Definition 

35.1.1 A double foul is a situation in which 2 opponents commit personal, disruptive, flagrant or disqualifying fouls on each other at approximately the same time. 

35.1.2 To consider 2 fouls as a double foul the following conditions must apply: • Both fouls are player fouls. • Both fouls involve physical contact. • Both fouls are between the same 2 opponents fouling each other. • Both fouls are either 2 personal or any combination of disruptive, flagrant and disqualifying fouls. 

35.2 Penalty A personal, disruptive, flagrant or disqualifying foul shall be charged against each offender. No free throws shall be awarded and the game shall be resumed as follows: If at approximately the same time as the double foul: • A valid goal, or a last free throw is scored, the ball shall be awarded to the non-scoring team for a throw-in from any place behind that team’s endline. • A team had control of the ball or was entitled to the ball, the ball shall be awarded to this team for a throw-in from the place nearest to the infraction. • Neither team had control of the ball nor was entitled to the ball, a jump ball situation occurs.
